# Limitations and Threats to Validity {#sec:limitations}

To keep PaperOEGM's claims strictly calibrated to empirical evidence, we highlight seven limitations and threats to validity.

<!-- run:exp01_faultbench_agg -->
**Self-Benchmark Circularity and Deterministic Scope in RQ1.** Because both `po_tools.py` and PO-FaultBench were co-authored, the 100.0% recall and 0.0% FPR on families FF1--FF8 verify implementation adherence to PaperOEGM's own schema rather than universal error detection on arbitrary papers. Moreover, four families test PaperOEGM-specific artifacts (`citation_ledger.json`, inline `run:<id>` comments, `check-readonly`), and unordered set matching in Equation~\eqref{eq:decimal-match} cannot catch metric-key swaps, unit-ambiguous $[0, 1]$ scaling, or untagged quantitative paragraphs. Pairing FF1--FF8 with 40 hard negative controls and 20 adversarial evasion cases (0.0% deterministic recall) exposes these boundaries explicitly; validation on external benchmarks such as PaperBench [@starace2025paperbench] or historical conference errata remains future work.

<!-- run:exp03_scalability_agg -->
**TeX Read-Sandbox Regression and Untested Prompt-Injection Defenses.** Static regex scanning of LaTeX sources misses macro-obfuscated shell escapes (0.0% recall on E2 in RQ1). Our RQ3 adversarial containment test confirmed that `--no-shell-escape` and `openout_any=p` block obfuscated `\csname write18\endcsname` execution (1.0) and parent-directory `\openout` writes (1.0), but revealed that upstream TeX Live 2026 made `openin_any=p` a silent no-op, permitting parent-directory `\openin` reads (0.0 blocked). Defending untrusted templates against arbitrary file exfiltration on TeX Live 2026+ therefore requires OS-level sandboxing (containers or `sandbox-exec`). Furthermore, while @schmotz2026skillinject show that skill-file prompt injections succeed at up to 80% ASR without context-aware authorization, we did not empirically test adaptive prompt injection inside scraped abstracts or PDFs against our read-only critics; read-only isolation and human gates bound write blast radius but do not guarantee immunity against finding suppression.

<!-- run:exp04_hybrid_agg -->
**Scope and Missing Baselines in RQ4.** Under our zero-external-API-key constraint, RQ4 evaluates $n=3$ compact (~150--180 word) seeded domain manuscripts (30 planted flaws across 15 live subagent invocations) on a single host runtime and model (Antigravity / Gemini 4), and omits both a same-context self-review baseline and a single-agent monolithic fresh critic ablation. Achieving 100.0% critic recall reflects dense, unambiguous flaws on short drafts; subtler methodological defects in full 9-page manuscripts with 80+ references will exhibit lower recall, consistent with the human-vs.-LLM overlap findings of @liang2024feedback. Our `clean_control_fp` check also audits only targeted control tokens rather than a full defect-free manuscript.

**Asymmetric Cross-Harness Verification.** Antigravity execution (all 10 skills and 5 live read-only critics) and Claude Code CLI packaging (`claude plugin validate`, marketplace registration, and plugin install) were live-verified. Codex support is structurally verified through synchronized manifests and deterministic ZIP packaging tests, but has not yet been live-executed in an interactive Codex CLI session.

<!-- run:exp03_scalability_agg -->
**Practical Runtime Quirks Observed During Dogfooding.** Four engineering friction points surfaced during evaluation and self-hosting: (1) invoking `po_tools.py` as four separate Python processes incurs ~266 ms of cold startup and PATH-probing overhead regardless of scale (`266.00` to `268.55 ms`); (2) `acmart` v2.16 populates a generic non-identifying `Creator: LaTeX with acmart ...` string via `hyperxmp` even with `\hypersetup{pdfcreator={}}`; (3) substring guards on `dry_run` paths and narrative `@` regexes can match literal documentation mentions in meta-papers describing PaperOEGM itself; and (4) `check-readonly` currently tracks macOS Finder `.DS_Store` metadata files unless explicitly ignored alongside `.git` and `__pycache__`.

**Human Accountability and Authorship Scope.** PaperOEGM is strictly a human-in-the-loop authoring aid (`disable-model-invocation: true`), not an autonomous paper mill. Human researchers remain accountable for scientific validity, Institutional Review Board (IRB) and ethics compliance, real experiment execution, and final verification of every citation and metric before submission.
